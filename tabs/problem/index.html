The Coral City Camera (CCC) community needs a more active, youth-driven way to connect real-time marine observation with education and conservation action. Right now, while the livestream exists, there is no structured system that helps students consistently identify species, track changes, or contribute usable data about fish health and population decline.

This matters locally because Miami’s coral reef ecosystems are directly affected by rising sea temperatures and algal blooms, meaning the decline of fish populations is not just a distant issue but one happening in our own waters. Without community engagement and accessible tools for learning and reporting, awareness stays passive instead of turning into meaningful environmental action.

Thank You Coral City Camera!

"The Coral City Camera provides a fish-eye view into the urban marine ecosystem that has developed around the human-made shorelines of Miami. The project was launched with the idea that the incredible biodiversity living just below the waterline in Miami should be a point of civic pride that engages the public to protect what they know and love. It is located along the shoreline at the east end of PortMiami in about 9' (3m) of water. It was deployed in late 2019 by Coral Morphologic as a hybrid art-science research project."
